## Spanish-specific translation rules

## Voice and Typography

- Use natural, broadly understandable Spanish for developers. Address the reader as "tú", usually omitting the pronoun. Avoid stiff constructions such as "permítanos" and literal English sentence order.
- Use sentence case, not English title case. Do not end headings, subheadings, navigation items, buttons, or short feature labels with a period, even when the English source does. Keep normal sentence punctuation in descriptions and paragraphs, and preserve meaningful question or exclamation marks with their opening marks.
- Prefer a clear verb or benefit to an abstract noun phrase. Do not copy repeated "more" constructions or translate English metaphors word for word.

## Developer Vocabulary

- "Shipping" can remain "shipping" in conversational developer copy, especially in established phrases such as "hacer shipping". Do not use the plural "shippings". In prominent marketing headings, prefer a natural expression such as "publicar mejoras" or "entregar software" when it communicates the benefit more clearly.
- Do not translate generic "shipping" as "lanzamientos" or "envíos". "Lanzamiento" is appropriate only when the source actually refers to a product launch or release event, not continuous delivery of changes.
- Use "compilar" and "compilación" for the process and its records, and the established loanword "build" for an app artifact when that is clearer. For example, "reduce los tiempos de compilación" reads more naturally than "reduce los tiempos de build". Use "ejecución" for a run.
- Keep established terms such as "tests", "feedback", "CI", and "caché" consistent. Use "entornos para agentes de IA" or "entornos para agentes de programación" rather than "entornos agénticos" when referring to coding agents' environments.
- Use "tests de UI" and "tests unitarios", not English compounds such as "UI y unit tests". A "runner" is a CI execution environment; keep the developer loanword when clearer, not "corredor". A billing "seat" is a "licencia" or "usuario", not an "asiento".
- "All systems normal" can be "Todos los sistemas funcionan con normalidad", not "Todos los sistemas operativos". For selective testing misses, explain that modules had to execute rather than calling them failed tests. "Fallo de caché" means data was not found, not that the service failed.

## Marketing Examples

- The hero's two entries, "Less waiting." and "More shipping.", are one message displayed on separate lines. A developer-oriented option is "Menos esperas" / "Más shipping"; alternatives include "Menos esperas" / "Más tiempo para entregar software". Keep each entry to its own headline line, without a final period. Avoid "Menos espera. Más lanzamientos." and "Más shippings".
- For "More in the toolbox.", prefer "Más herramientas para tu equipo" or "Más funcionalidades para tu día a día". Avoid "Más en la caja de herramientas", which does not explain the section naturally.
- Read split headings together: "Never build the" / "same thing twice." can be "No compiles" / "lo mismo dos veces"; "Faster tests you can" / "actually trust." can be "Tests más rápidos" / "en los que puedes confiar". Preserve the message across the slots, not English word order.
- For a count followed by "Active community members and counting", use "Miembros activos en una comunidad que sigue creciendo", not "Miembros activos de la comunidad y sigue creciendo". Make agreement and the subject of growth explicit.
