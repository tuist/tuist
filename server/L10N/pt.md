## Portuguese-specific translation rules

## Voice and Typography

- Write natural Portuguese for developers. The target is `pt`, not explicitly `pt-BR` or `pt-PT`: both established regional variants are acceptable. Keep register and vocabulary consistent within a page; do not silently normalize the whole corpus to one region.
- For example, "faturamento", "assinatura", "equipe", and "baixar" form a Brazilian vocabulary set; "faturação", "subscrição", "equipa", and "transferir" form a European set. Do not alternate sets arbitrarily within one experience.
- Use sentence case and omit final periods in headings, navigation, buttons, and short labels. Retain normal sentence punctuation in paragraphs. Prefer clear verbs and natural agreement to literal English word order.

## Developer Vocabulary and Marketing

- A newsletter issue is an "edição", not a "problema". A percentile is a "percentil" (plural "percentis"), not a percentage. "Falha de cache" means requested data was not found for reuse, not a failed build; preserve miss reasons, such as "por alterações" or "por remoção de itens".
- Keep "Air", "Pro", and "Enterprise" unchanged as pricing-plan names; "Air" is not "Ar" in this context. Translate all claims in customer quotes without dropping the final sentence to shorten them.
- "Shipping" means delivering software or publishing updates, not freight. For "More in the toolbox", a direction such as "Mais ferramentas para o seu dia a dia" conveys additional capabilities without a physical-box metaphor.
- Read split headings as one sentence: "Never build the" / "same thing twice." can be "Nunca compile" / "a mesma coisa duas vezes", without a final period. Do not leave an extra article at the end of the first fragment.
