## Japanese-specific translation rules

## Voice and Typography

- Write natural Japanese for software developers. Omit unnecessary subjects and possessives such as "あなた" and "私たちの". Avoid literal English word order and overly formal sales language.
- Use concise noun phrases or natural verb phrases for headings, navigation, and buttons. Omit the final "。" or ASCII period in these elements, even if the English heading has one. Use consistent "です・ます" prose and normal Japanese punctuation in descriptions and paragraphs.
- Do not imitate English capitalization or insert spaces between every Japanese word. Preserve the spelling and casing of product names and acronyms.

## Developer Vocabulary and Marketing

- Use established terms such as "ビルド", "テスト", "キャッシュ", "実行", and "依存関係の解決". For product capabilities, "features" means "機能", not the general "特徴".
- "Shipping" means delivering software or updates, usually "リリース" or "提供" in context, not "配送" or "出荷". Do not preserve English "shipping" just because it is common in English developer copy.
- For "Less waiting. More shipping.", a natural direction is "待ち時間を減らし、開発にもっと集中". For "More in the toolbox", prefer "チームを支える、さらに多くの機能" to a literal reference to a toolbox.
- Read split headings as one sentence: "Never build the" / "same thing twice." should express "同じものを二度ビルドしない", not two independent assertions. A newsletter issue is a "号", not a "問題".
- Preserve billing meaning: traffic counted at half consumes half the usage allowance; it does not automatically cost half the price. Translate ordinary connective prose instead of leaving words such as "wherever" or "allowance" in otherwise Japanese sentences.
