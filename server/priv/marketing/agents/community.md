# Tuist community

Where to ask questions, discuss build tooling, and contribute to Tuist.

## Where to go

- [Community forum](https://community.tuist.dev): searchable questions, answers, and discussions. The default support channel on the free plan.
- [Community Slack](https://slack.tuist.dev): conversations with users and the team, and where to request Tuist Runners access.
- [Source repository](https://github.com/tuist/tuist): issues and contributions. Follow its contribution guidelines.
- [Full community page](/marketing-markdown/source/community): ambassadors, translators, and alumni who contributed.

## Asking for help effectively

Include the toolchain and Tuist versions, whether the project is generated, the expected and actual behavior, and a minimal reproduction. Remove tokens, credentials, and private project data from logs before posting.

## Limitations

Community channels have no guaranteed response time and are not a paid support agreement; Pro and Enterprise support channels are described under [pricing](/marketing-markdown/pricing). Report security vulnerabilities privately, as described in [Security](/marketing-markdown/security), never in a public issue.
