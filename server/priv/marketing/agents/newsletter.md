# Tuist Digest newsletter

An entry point to the Tuist team's periodic newsletter: product updates, perspective on app development, and where the product is heading.

## What this area provides

The [newsletter page](/newsletter) offers email subscription and an archive of past issues. **Current entries** at the end of this guide links up to 20 recent issues, newest first; the [sitemap](/sitemap.xml) covers the rest. Request an issue URL with `Accept: text/markdown` for its full content.

## When to use it

Use it for context and direction. Use the [changelog](/marketing-markdown/changelog) for dated shipped changes and the documentation for setup.

## Limitations

Plans described in an issue are not shipped capabilities or committed dates. Subscribing involves a person's email address and requires their consent; do not subscribe anyone on their behalf without it, and never reuse confirmation or verification links. See [privacy](/privacy).
