# Tuist: company and mission

Tuist is build infrastructure for engineering teams and coding agents. Shared infrastructure connects building, testing, and review so people and agents can work together with reliable feedback.

## Mission and approach

Automation only moves as fast as the loop it runs in. Every developer, and every agent working alongside them, needs fast, reliable feedback. Tuist connects shared caching, build and test insights, and app previews to help teams move from a change to something they can test and review. Capabilities can be adopted independently; support and prerequisites vary by feature.

## Background

Tuist started as an open-source developer toolkit and grew into a business building shared infrastructure for engineering teams. The founders are Pedro Piñera (CEO) and Marek Fořt (CTO). The team publishes how it works in the [company handbook](https://handbook.tuist.dev).
