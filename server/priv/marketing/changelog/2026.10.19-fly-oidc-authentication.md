---
title: Authenticate workloads from any OpenID Connect provider
description: Trust identity tokens from Fly.io Machines and other OpenID Connect providers without storing long-lived Tuist secrets.
category: "Product"
---

Tuist accounts can now trust workloads from any OpenID Connect identity provider that publishes its signing keys over HTTPS, in addition to GitHub Actions, CircleCI, and Bitrise. In the **Workload identities** section of your account's **Settings → Authentication** page, configure the provider's issuer URL, its JWKS URL, the audience, and the exact claims a token must carry, then add the trust policy. Saved identities appear as cards with their configuration details visible and individual removal controls. Configure an identity once for organization-wide CI access to all current and future projects in the account, without selecting projects individually. Only workloads whose tokens are signed by that provider and match every claim can exchange them for short-lived CI access; the token cannot administer the account or access another account's projects.

Fly.io Machines work out of the box: trust your Fly organization's issuer with its `org_id` and `app_id` claims, request an identity token with audience `tuist` from the Machine's local Fly API, and pass it through `TUIST_OIDC_TOKEN` to `tuist auth login` or `mix tuist.login`. Set `TUIST_OIDC_ACCOUNT` to the Tuist account that owns the project; it's required for every trust policy exchange. No repository connection or long-lived Tuist secret is needed.

See the [workload identity guide](/en/docs/guides/server/authentication#workload-identity-trust-policies) and the [Fly.io example](/en/docs/guides/server/authentication#flyio-machines). Tuist sessions last one hour without refresh tokens. Removing a policy stops new exchanges; already-issued Tuist access tokens remain valid until they expire.
