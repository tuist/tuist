---
title: See where your Xcode build spends its time
description: "Explore Xcode builds on an interactive timeline. See which steps overlap, zoom into slow operations, and inspect their logs directly in the dashboard."
category: "Product"
---

A build's total duration tells you how long you waited. The new **Timeline** tab shows how that time was spent, laying out compilation, linking, scripts, and resource processing across targets so you can see which steps ran together and which operations took the longest.

![The Xcode build timeline in the Tuist dashboard, showing overlapping build steps and a long Swift module emission for TuistServer within a 21.3-second build](/marketing/images/changelog/2026.09.05-xcode-build-timeline.png)

Pinch to zoom, drag across a time range to focus, or double-click a step to take a closer look. Search for a step or target to find the work you care about, and move the cursor across the chart to read its exact time.

Select a step to inspect its target, type, start time, duration, and outcome alongside its recorded log. You can go from spotting a long operation to reading its command and output in the same view. Drag the divider to give the log more room; the selected step stays open as you explore the rest of the build.

Timelines use the standard Xcode activity log already uploaded with build insights, with no extra Xcode timing setting required. Open **Build Runs**, choose a newly processed Xcode build, and select **Timeline**.
