---
title: Get an email before reaching your Air limits
description: "Air accounts now receive email notifications at 80% and 100% of their monthly remote cache and runner allowances, with usage details, the reset date, and a link to upgrade."
category: "Product"
---

Air now emails every organization admin when the daily usage refresh finds that the account has reached **80%** or **100%** of either its monthly remote cache allowance or its monthly runner allowance. Personal accounts receive the notification at the owner's email address.

The 80% email gives you time to review usage and upgrade to Pro before remote cache access pauses. Each email shows how much of the allowance you've used, when it resets, and a link to billing.

![Air usage warning email showing 160 of 200 remote cache hits used and a link to review usage and upgrade](/marketing/images/changelog/2026.09.09-air-usage-80.png)

At 100%, the email explains that remote cache access is paused until you upgrade or the free allowance resets. Pro includes the same free allowance, with usage-based pricing beyond it.

![Air limit-reached email showing 200 of 200 remote cache hits used and the allowance reset date](/marketing/images/changelog/2026.09.09-air-usage-100.png)

Runner emails follow the same thresholds: **80** and **100 baseline runner minutes** with the default Air allowance. They show runner usage and explain that new runner jobs pause when the allowance is exhausted. Active runner trials are excluded because their usage is not capped.

![Air runner warning email showing 80 of 100 baseline runner minutes used](/marketing/images/changelog/2026.09.09-air-runner-80.png)

![Air runner limit-reached email showing that new jobs pause until the allowance resets or the account upgrades](/marketing/images/changelog/2026.09.09-air-runner-100.png)

Each admin receives each threshold notification once per allowance and usage period. Cache and runner usage are tracked separately, and runner notifications reset at the start of each UTC calendar month. If usage jumps past both thresholds between refreshes, only the 100% email is sent.
