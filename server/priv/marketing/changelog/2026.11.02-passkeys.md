---
title: Sign in with passkeys
description: "Sign in to Tuist with a passkey from 1Password, iCloud Keychain, Google Password Manager, Windows Hello, or a security key."
category: "Product"
---

You can now sign in to Tuist with a **passkey** instead of a password. A passkey is unlocked with your fingerprint, face, or device PIN, and it only works on Tuist's own site, so it can't be phished.

To add one, open **Account Settings → Authentication → Manage two-factor authentication** and click **Add a passkey**. Your browser or password manager, such as 1Password, iCloud Keychain, Google Password Manager, or Windows Hello, stores it for you. You can add several passkeys, see when each was last used, and remove any you no longer need.

Then pick **Log in with a passkey** on the login page, or choose your passkey from the email field's autofill. A passkey sign-in already confirms both possession of your device and your identity, so it also satisfies two-factor authentication. If you sign in with a password, GitHub, Google, or SSO and have the authenticator app enabled, you can use a passkey instead of the six-digit code.

Organizations that require single sign-on still require it: a passkey doesn't replace your company's SSO.
