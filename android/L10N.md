# Tuist Android App Translation Context

The Tuist Android app lists a project's previews, which are app builds shared by a team, and installs them on the device.

## Catalog format

- The source is an Android `strings.xml` resource file. Translate only the quoted text inside each `<string>` element; keep every `name` attribute unchanged.
- Locale codes are Android resource qualifiers because they name the `values-*` directories the app loads. `zh-rCN` is Simplified Chinese, `zh-rTW` is Traditional Chinese, and `yue-rHK` is Cantonese written with Traditional Chinese characters. Android 7 and later match these to devices by script, not only by region.
- Preserve every format argument exactly, such as `%1$d` and `%1$s`, and escapes such as `\n`, `\"`, and `\'`.

## Meaning

- A "preview" is a shareable app build that can be installed and run, not a visual preview or a draft. "Run" installs and opens that build on the device.
- "Threads" is the name of Tuist's private AI coding feature. A "thread" is a conversation where someone describes work, reviews changes, and approves shipping them. Keep the product name "Threads" unchanged.
- Error messages are shown in snackbars and empty states. Keep them short, helpful, and non-blaming.

## Right-to-left locales

- The app mirrors its layout for Arabic automatically. Do not add invisible bidirectional control characters (such as U+200F or U+202B) to translations; direction belongs to the rendering layer.
- Keep Latin product names, platform names, identifiers, and format arguments in their original spelling. Place a format argument where the target sentence needs it; the system orders mixed-direction text.
