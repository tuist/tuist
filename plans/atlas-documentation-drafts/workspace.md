## Customer relationships

Accounts and contacts provide a shared place for customer context. Sales conversations, evaluations, licenses, and contracts can relate to these records so people can follow the relationship over time.

## Projects and knowledge

Use projects, specifications, notes, and tasks to keep decisions close to the work. Assign tasks to Atlas users and connect them to an account when that context matters.

## Documents and finance

Documents bring uploaded material into the workspace. Finance workflows can classify invoices and extract their information when the required providers are configured. Configure storage and extraction before relying on these workflows.

## Communication and support

Email and support workflows connect conversations to customer records. Delivery and ingestion require provider configuration. Review the sending identity and destinations before enabling them for your organization.

## Permissions and audit

Users receive access through roles and scopes. Administrator access is explicit, starting with the [first administrator command](/docs/first-administrator). Atlas records internal operations in its audit log.

The ongoing self-hosting work includes reviewing permission enforcement across all domains. Treat the current deployment as an organizational workspace and review access before exposing sensitive workflows.
